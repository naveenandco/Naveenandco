FAQs

Common questions

Have a question that isn’t covered here? Send us an enquiry and tell us a little about your requirement.

What services does Naveen & Co. provide?

Services include GST registration and return support, income tax return filing, TDS services, PAN/TAN assistance, business registrations, labour and professional tax compliance, accounting support and related documentation. Confirm your exact requirement with us.

What documents should I keep ready?

Documents depend on the service and your circumstances. For an initial discussion, you can tell us which service you need; we’ll then outline the relevant information and documents. Avoid sending sensitive identity or financial documents through an unsecured channel.

Can you help individuals as well as businesses?

Yes. We provide support for individual tax filing requirements and business tax and compliance matters. The scope of assistance depends on the facts of each case.

How do I confirm fees and timelines?

Contact us with your requirement. Fees and timelines can vary depending on the service, complexity, documents available and applicable statutory deadlines. We’ll discuss the scope before proceeding.

What are your office hours?

Monday to Friday: 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Saturday: 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Sunday: Closed. Public holiday hours may vary; please contact us before visiting.
